DEFINING THE ASSIGNMENT

Commercial properties & facilities

Begin with the type of property, hours of operation, occupancy, and the responsibilities expected of an unarmed security presence. Discuss fixed-post coverage, visitor interaction, and coordination with property management.

Access control & private events

Identify authorized entry points, visitor procedures, guest volumes, event schedules, and the customer’s escalation contacts. The scope should make the relationship between security, venue staff, and event management clear.

Patrol observation & documentation

Define the areas to be observed, reporting intervals, and the information property managers need. Incident documentation should distinguish observed facts, actions taken, and required follow-up.

WHAT TO INCLUDE IN YOUR INQUIRY

Location & operating environment

Share the site location, property type, normal hours, and any access or occupancy considerations.

Coverage & timing

Describe the requested dates, coverage hours, expected duration, and whether the requirement is recurring or event-specific.

Reporting & coordination

Identify the decision-maker, site contact, and any established site instructions or reporting expectations.

Customer inquiries

Include the location, property or event type, intended coverage hours, target dates, and a brief description of the requirement. Business inquiries

Include your organization, area of expertise or investment interest, and the business relationship you would like to explore. Describe the strategic fit, the market or opportunity involved, and any relevant timeframe. A brief introduction is enough to begin; detailed materials and further questions can be discussed once the purpose of the conversation is clear.

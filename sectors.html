SECTOR REQUIREMENTS

Commercial property & management

Commercial properties bring together tenants, visitors, contractors, and deliveries throughout the day. Security discussions should address shared spaces, entry procedures, opening and closing routines, and the management team’s expectations for communication. Clear boundaries between security duties and property-management responsibilities help keep the assignment practical.

Warehouses & distribution facilities

Warehouses and distribution facilities have distinct patterns of arrivals, departures, and activity. Define gate and visitor procedures, authorized access, contractor arrivals, and observation areas alongside the normal operating schedule. The inquiry should identify how security would coordinate with facility personnel and where activity needs to be documented.

Business parks & offices

Business parks and offices may combine multiple occupants, shared entrances, reception teams, and after-hours access. A site discussion should consider building entry, common areas, parking observations, and the communication chain between occupants and property management. Changes in occupancy or work schedules may also affect coverage requirements.

Private events

Private events require a defined relationship between organizers, venue personnel, and the security assignment. Discuss guest entry, approved access areas, arrival volumes, event schedules, and points of contact. Identifying responsibilities before the event supports orderly coordination when timing, attendance, or venue conditions change.
